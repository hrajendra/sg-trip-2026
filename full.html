🇸🇬 Full Family Itinerary

6 – 10 April 2026 · 8 People

Before You Board
Pre-Trip Checklist ✈️
⚠️ Do this week — 20 days out
  • Travel insurance — all 8 people. Check Chase Sapphire / Amex Platinum card benefits first (may cover medical emergencies). Otherwise World Nomads, Allianz, or Seven Corners. ~$150–300 USD for the group. Grandparents' Indian health insurance does not cover Singapore.
  • Grandparent SIM cards — buy Airtel International or Singtel SIM at BLR airport before they board, OR pick up Singtel at Changi T2 arrivals and hand over on meeting. Without data they cannot WhatsApp you or access the guide during the 55-minute arrival gap.
  • Google Photos shared album — create it now, add all 8 people. Help grandparents install the app and join before departure.
😴 Sleep shift — start 2 weeks out (Mar 23)

SFO → Singapore is +15 hours — a full day inversion. The kids' bodies will think it's 3 AM when you land at 6:35 PM. Start shifting their bedtime 15 minutes earlier every 2 days from Mar 23. Target: 1–1.5 hours ahead of normal SFO schedule by Apr 5.

  • Mar 23: bedtime 15 min earlier than normal
  • Mar 25: 30 min earlier
  • Mar 27: 45 min earlier
  • Mar 29: 1 hr earlier
  • Mar 31+: 1–1.5 hrs earlier — hold here until departure

Grandparents (BLR→SIN) are only +2.5 hours — mild adjustment, mostly travel fatigue.

✈️ In-flight strategy (15+ hours)
  • Keep kids awake for the first half of the flight (daytime Singapore time)
  • Aim for sleep in the second half (night Singapore time)
  • Time snacks to Singapore meal times, not hunger
  • Melatonin for the 4yr old (paediatrician-approved dose) — signals sleep on the plane
  • No screens after the sleep window starts — white noise app instead
🌡️ Weather prep — 32°C + 80–90% humidity

SFO in April is ~15°C. Singapore is 32°C with tropical humidity — a 17°C jump. Bodies take 3–4 days to acclimatise. Days 1 and 2 everyone will tire faster and feel the heat more than expected.

  • Pack light cotton only — no synthetic fabrics
  • Indoor cooling breaks are physiologically necessary, not optional — the schedule builds these in deliberately
  • Sunscreen every morning before leaving the hotel, reapply at lunch
  • Carry a small water bottle per person — hawker centres don't always sell water
  • 👶 Toddler hydration: offer water every 60–90 minutes — they dehydrate faster than they communicate thirst
  • 👶 Zinc oxide cream (prickly heat / sweat rash) — pack in day bag, common for toddlers in nappies in this climate
🎒 Pack before you leave
  • Grandparents' medications — confirm they have enough for 5 days + 2–3 days buffer, in their hand luggage. Singapore has Guardian and Watsons everywhere but prescription meds may not be available.
  • Baby carrier — needed for Cloud Forest (loud sounds, Day 5). Pack in Day 5 day bag specifically, not in checked luggage.
  • White noise app — install and test before travel. Helps kids sleep in unfamiliar hotel rooms.
  • Quiet activity bag for the hotel room — sticker books, small Duplo, no lights or noise. For 2–4 AM jet lag wake-ups in Days 2–3.
  • Paediatric medicine kit — Paracetamol drops, antihistamine, ORS sachets, zinc oxide cream. Singapore has Guardian and Watsons everywhere but avoid a mid-sightseeing pharmacy run.
  • Slip-on shoes for the adults on temple days (Days 2 and 3) — shoes off outside, 32°C heat, elderly grandparents.
  • Covered shoulders + knees for Sultan Mosque Day 2 — check grandfathers' packing specifically.
  • Toddler formula/food — bring enough for the trip. Nearest FairPrice Finest: Velocity@Novena, 10 min by MRT from hotel.
  • 1 Second Everyday app — install now and film 5 seconds each day. Set a reminder on Day 1 before dinner.
📋 Logistics to confirm before Apr 3
  • SG Arrival Card — file at eservices.ica.gov.sg/sgarrivalcard/ within 3 days of Apr 6 (file Apr 3–6)
  • IndiGo check-in — goindigo.in, opens 48 hrs before Apr 10 7:40 PM departure
  • Google Fi — upgrade to Unlimited Premium before leaving SFO
  • Rang Mahal — call to reconfirm table for 8 + high chair, give them your mobile number
  • Download Grab + add US credit card (do at home, easier before travel)
  • Grandparents visa approval confirmation — appointment was Mar 20 Chennai
😴 Jet lag — what to expect, night by night

Your bodies land at 6:35 PM Singapore time thinking it's 3:35 AM SFO time. Here's the honest night-by-night picture:

  • Night 1 (Apr 6) — everyone crashes easily. Actually fine. ✅
  • Night 2 (Apr 7) — kids likely wake 1–3 AM. Day 3 ahead (the longest day). ⚠️
  • Night 3 (Apr 8) — wakes 3–5 AM. Getting better. ⚠️
  • Night 4 (Apr 9) — wakes 5–6 AM. Nearly adjusted. ✅

Night 2 plan — if kids are up at 2 AM: no screens, no lights, minimal stimulation. Quiet activity bag. Try for back to sleep. Do NOT let anyone nap after 4 PM on Days 1–3 however tempting — late naps destroy night sleep.
